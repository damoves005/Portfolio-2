About Me

Bio

Jan Kasper Udaundo

Web Developer

Hello World! I’m Jan Kasper Udaundo, a Web Developer and Code Instructor based in Edmonton, Alberta. I enjoy creating websites that are clean, easy to use, and look great on any device. I’ve worked on e-commerce sites, personal portfolios, and educational projects that help people learn and connect online.

I started my career teaching computer skills and programming to students. That experience helped me understand how to explain technical ideas in a simple way and focus on how people use technology every day.

I like solving problems, learning new tools, and finding better ways to design and build things on the web. My goal is to grow as a developer, work with creative people, and keep improving my skills while building projects that make a difference.
