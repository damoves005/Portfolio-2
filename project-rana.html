Rana Pharmaceutical Website

Professional pharmaceutical information portal for healthcare providers

Personal Project HTML • CSS • JavaScript • PHP
January 2022

Project Initiation

Received requirements from Rana Pharmaceutical to create a professional website showcasing their products and company information. The goal was to establish their online presence and provide resources for healthcare professionals.

  • Met with stakeholders to gather requirements
  • Analyzed pharmaceutical industry web standards
  • Defined project scope and deliverables
  • Created project timeline and milestones
February 2022

Information Architecture

Organized product information, company history, and regulatory content into a logical, easily navigable structure suitable for healthcare professionals.

  • Structured product catalog and categories
  • Organized company information and history
  • Planned contact and inquiry forms
  • Created site map and navigation flow
February 2022

Professional Design

Developed a clean, professional design that instills trust and credibility. Used medical industry color schemes and typography that reflect pharmaceutical standards.

  • Created professional brand identity
  • Designed trust-building layouts
  • Developed product showcase templates
  • Ensured regulatory compliance in design

  • Added PDF document downloads

Testing & Quality Assurance
